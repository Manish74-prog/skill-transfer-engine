# Career Transition Paths & Roadmaps

## 1. Sales Executive → Product Manager
- **Existing Skills:** Communication, Customer Understanding, Negotiation
- **Learn:** Product Analytics, Product Strategy, Agile
- **Build:** Product Case Study

### Roadmap
- **LEARN:** Product Strategy, Product Analytics, Agile, User Research
- **PRACTICE:** Analyze existing products, Write PRDs, Competitor analysis
- **BUILD:** Product case study, PRD, Product improvement proposal
- **APPLY:** Product internships, APM roles

---

## 2. Sales Executive → Customer Success Manager
- **Existing Skills:** Customer Handling, Communication, CRM
- **Learn:** Customer Retention, Customer Success Strategy
- **Practice:** Customer Success Case Study

### Roadmap
- **LEARN:** Customer Retention, Customer Success Metrics, Account Management
- **PRACTICE:** Customer Onboarding Plans, Escalation Handling
- **BUILD:** Customer Success Case Study & Playbook
- **APPLY:** Customer Success Manager roles

---

## 3. Python Developer → Data Analyst
- **Existing Skills:** Python, Problem Solving, Basic Data Handling
- **Learn:** SQL, Statistics, Data Visualization
- **Build:** Data Analysis Dashboard

### Roadmap
- **LEARN:** SQL, Statistics, Power BI / Tableau
- **PRACTICE:** Data cleaning on Kaggle datasets, Writing SQL queries
- **BUILD:** End-to-end Data Analysis Dashboard
- **APPLY:** Entry-level Data Analyst roles

---

## 4. Business Analyst → Product Manager
- **Existing Skills:** Data Analysis, Communication, Requirement Gathering
- **Learn:** Product Strategy, Agile, User Research
- **Build:** Product Case Study

### Roadmap
- **LEARN:** Product Strategy, Agile, Product Metrics
- **PRACTICE:** Wireframing, User Journey Mapping
- **BUILD:** Product Requirement Document (PRD)
- **APPLY:** Product Manager roles
